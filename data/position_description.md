Position Title: Senior Product Analyst

Company Overview:
At NovaCore Solutions, we are driven by innovation, collaboration, and integrity. We believe in building technology that empowers people and businesses to make smarter decisions. Our culture values curiosity, ownership, transparency, and continuous learning. We strive to create an inclusive environment where diverse perspectives lead to better outcomes.

Position Summary:
The Senior Product Analyst plays a key role in driving data-informed decisions across product development. This professional will work closely with cross-functional teams including Product, Engineering, Marketing, and Sales to analyze user behavior, identify opportunities, and optimize product performance.

Core Responsibilities:

Analyze product performance metrics and generate actionable insights
Collaborate with product managers to define KPIs and success metrics
Design and maintain dashboards and reports for stakeholders
Conduct user behavior analysis and A/B testing
Translate complex data into clear business recommendations
Support product strategy through market and competitor analysis
Ensure data accuracy and integrity across all reporting systems

Required Knowledge and Skills:

Strong proficiency in data analysis tools (SQL, Python, or R)
Experience with data visualization tools (Tableau, Power BI, or similar)
Solid understanding of product lifecycle and agile methodologies
Analytical thinking with strong problem-solving abilities
Excellent communication and storytelling skills with data
Ability to work collaboratively in a fast-paced environment

Preferred Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Data Science, Business, Engineering, or related field
3+ years of experience in product analytics or similar role
Experience with experimentation platforms and statistical analysis
